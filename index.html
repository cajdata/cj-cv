2025 Site Exit & Consolidation Program
Uniti Group · 2025
Problem
Uniti's network footprint included sites generating unnecessary monthly recurring costs. Safely exiting them required coordinating circuit grooming across dozens of facilities without disrupting active customer handoffs.
Action
Planned and executed 7 site exits and 3 site consolidations. Decommissioned 57 circuits and groomed 634 circuits to alternate paths, validating every customer handoff before cutover.
Impact
$261,000 in monthly recurring costs eliminated across all 10 projects with zero customer-impacting events.

Truth or Consequences Node Insert
Uniti Group · Albuquerque Consolidation
Problem
A site consolidation in Albuquerque required relocating a Cyan Z77 node that was the sole feed for broadband service to Truth or Consequences, NM. Taking it offline risked an outage of unknown duration if the node didn't come back up cleanly.
Action
Identified the risk ahead of the move and performed a node insert, placing a second Z77 between the feeder and Truth or Consequences. Groomed all broadband traffic to the inserted node before the relocation began.
Impact
Zero service impact to the city during the move. Consolidation completed on schedule with all customers unaffected.
